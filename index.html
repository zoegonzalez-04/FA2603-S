COMIC BOOK
HUNTER

Somewhere in this city, the rarest comics are turning up on dusty shelves. You're a collector, and every night you get one shot to track one down.

How the hunt works

YOUR BRIEFING

THE HUNT

Each night, one rare comic is out there. You can visit one shop to try to get it. Your goal is to give yourself the best chance of getting the comic.

THE MAP

You'll see a map of five comic shops. Each shop shows the chance it has tonight's comic in stock.

Each shop has one copy.

OTHER COLLECTORS ARE HUNTING TOO

Three other collectors hunt the same comic every night. They always go to the shop with the highest chance.

If you go to the same shop as other collectors and it has the comic, the one copy goes to one of you, chosen at random. You might get it, or one of them might.

NO OTHER COLLECTORS

You're the only collector hunting tonight. No one else will visit any of the shops.

FIVE TRIALS

You'll make one choice in each of five trials. You won't see a result between trials.

HOW TO PLAY

Click a shop to select it, then press HUNT!
